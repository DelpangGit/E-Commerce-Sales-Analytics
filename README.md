# E-Commerce-Sales-Analytics
E-commerce financial analysis report (KAGGLE)


Source: Kaggle


Dataset

Star schema consisting of:

Sales (Fact Table)
DateTable
order_items
product_catalog
customer_master


Key DAX Measures:
Profit Margin
SalesNet % All Countries
SalesNet % All Regions
SalesNet YoY Growth
SalesNet YoY Growth %
SalesNetYTD
TotalNet_Sales
SalesOrderCount

ProductID Rank by Order Quantity
ProductID Rank by NetSales
ProductID Percent Rank Desc
ProductCategory Rank by NetSales
and more


Dashboard Features
Sales Trends
Customer Analysis
Product Analysis
Product Performance
Regional Analysis



Business questions answered by this report : 

-- What are the big numbers; revenue from net sales, product cost and number of total orders? 
-- What are the top 10 best-selling product categories? Compere their profits against the revenue
-- What is the fluctuation of profit in the course of time (in months) ?

-- What are the top 5 best-selling product categories and subcategories as per net sales?
-- What are the top 5 best-selling brands per net sales?
-- What are the top 10 best-selling brands per net sales?

-- Time intelligence
-- What is the Year Over Year (YoY) growth figure in net sales? What is the growth percentage?
-- What are the Year To Date (YTD) sales for each month?

-- Compare the countries and their related states as per their net sales
-- Compare the % of net sales for each state to their respective country
-- What is the running sum for each financial quarter?

-- Compare the profits for each fiscal year to their previous year
-- Calculate the running sum and the moving average for the profits for each fiscal year
-- Make a prediction for the net sales amounts for the next 2 months in 2022
-- Show the net sales and the relative profit margin for each month

-- Compare the customers by segments as per their respective net sales
-- Compare the customers by segments as per the customer age that produces the most profit
-- Compare the sales channels as per their respective net sales
-- Identify the customers and the customer segments who produced the most revenue
-- Identify the customer age groups which produced the most revenue and placed the highest amount of orders
-- Identify which marketing campaign produced the highest amount of revenue
-- Identify which marketing channel produced the highest amount of revenue
